政体

英文原名：Government

本文已针对游戏当前的PC版本（1.20）进行核实。

A government is the system that rules characters and their titles. It determines the laws available to a character as well as any obligations (taxes and levies) a vassal may have to their liege. Governments can only be changed via decisions. Governments are divided into three types:

  • Landed governments require the character to own landed titles. If a player character loses all their landed titles, they must choose to play as another dynasty member or receive a game over but if the  Roads to Power DLC is installed, they will have the option to choose between doing that or changing government to Adventurer. If a character with a landed government inherits a realm of higher rank they will adopt the realm's government.
  • Domicile governments give their house heads or potentially all rulers a domicile, which allows a player to play as them even if they do not own landed titles. If a ruler changes their government to a landed one, their domicile is sold for  Gold, all title men-at-arms are removed, and if their government used  Treasury or  Herd they are converted to  Gold as well. If a character with a domicile government inherits a realm of higher rank they will not change government.
  • Non-dynastic governments do not use dynastic succession and as a result are not playable. When a character with a non-dynastic government dies another random character will inherit all titles. If a character with a non-dynastic government inherits a realm of higher rank they will not change government.

Landed governments

Tribal

Tribal governments have access to the Tribal Authority law, unlock Subjugate and Conquest casus belli, and can start a Co-Monarchy. All tribal rulers can raid unless their rite forbids it and gain 100 more  Prestige when they win a battle. However, they cannot construct holdings or use innovations beyond the Tribal Era. Tribal rulers can only use the Confederate Partition succession law but unlock the Challenge the Ruler decision. Tribal rulers have access to an inferior Royal Court.

Development only affects the Supply Limit and does not passively grow in counties ruled by a tribal ruler. Tribal baronies only provide half of their supply limit to armies belonging to other governments.

Tribal governments have the following unique effects:

  • Tribal rulers pay for men-at-arms using  Prestige instead of  Gold but the maintenance cost is reduced by 30%.
  • Tribal rulers cannot imprison vassals or courtiers without passing level 2 Tribal Authority.
  • Tribal vassals provide taxes and levies based on the Liege's Level of Fame.
  • −50% Title creation cost.
  • +0.2 monthly  Prestige.
  • −20  Opinion with vassals that do not have  Tribal,  Wanua or  Nomadic government

Feudal

Feudal governments can create new holdings and cadet branches, have access to the Crown Authority law, can apply Title Succession laws and can start a Co-Monarchy.

Clan

Clan governments can create new holdings and cadet branches and have access to the Crown Authority law. All powerful vassals will want to be in an Alliance with their liege and will gain -30  Opinion of their liege if they are not. When clan rulers are at peace, they can appoint a Vizier. House heads with this government also unlock interactions to give house members the  Extolled by House or  Accused of Decadence traits.

If a clan ruler is a Dynasty Head, their realm will be named after their dynasty rather than the primary title. This behavior can be disabled in game settings.

Tax Jurisdictions

原站图片
Tax Jurisdictions

Vassals with Clan government provide taxes and levies via Tax Jurisdictions. If a ruler has vassals with Clan government then the Tax Jurisdictions tab will become available on the sidebar. Each Tax Jurisdiction is made up of a  Tax Collector and up to 12 Taxpayers. Taxpayers are assigned automatically but can be manually added or removed afterwards. Count and Duke rulers can appoint 1  Tax Collector while King, Emperor and Hegemon rulers can appoint 2. Cultures with the Enlightened Magnates or Legalistic tradition can each appoint an additional one, as can dynasties with the first Brilliance legacy. A Vizier, depending on its Stewardship score, can grant between 1 and 5 additional  Tax Collector. Two Innovations, Magistrates and Court Officials, also each grant an additional one. First perk from Avaricious stewardship lifestyle tree - Golden obligation also grants one 1  Tax Collector. Only unlanded courtiers can be appointed as  Tax Collector and only if the rite's View on Gender doctrine allows it. House unity

原站图片
Impassive house unity

If a House Head has  Clan government, then its house will have an additional mechanic called House Unity, representing how well members of a house tend to get along with each other. It will be displayed for all house members but only those who have Clan government will be affected by its effects. House Unity is increased by taking Unifying Actions towards them and decreased by taking Divisive Actions towards them. House heads also unlock the Steer House Unity decision.

The House Head also gains access to interactions to give and remove from house members the  Extolled by House or  Accused of Decadence trait. Both interactions cost  Piety that scales with how many virtue and sin traits the targeted house member has. Each trait can only be given once per 3 years. If a character with one of these traits becomes House Head the trait is lost.

Theocratic

Theocratic governments are used by clergy members of rites that have the  Theocratic clerical tradition and their religion is not Christianity. Theocratic governments can appear in every game but are only playable if the  By God Alone DLC is installed.

Theocratic governments use the succession law with the same name and the Church Authority law, can hold  Temple holdings, do not use  Legitimacy, do not have access to Coronation laws, and their Realm Priest provide all their taxes and levies regardless of  Opinion.

Theocratic governments also do not have access to casus belli except Independence and Holy War but Heads of Rite whose level of devotion is at least  Faithful unlock the Incite Claim War and Incite Holy War interactions, which cost 100  Piety per title rank of the targeted character. The potential attacker must accept it and acceptance can be increased by offering to join the war, pay scaled  Gold, spend additional  Piety, or use a hook.

If their liege follows a different faith, Theocratic vassals provide their liege with 10% of their Taxes  Levies. If the liege follows the same faith the provided taxes and levies depend on the liege's level of devotion.

Mandala

仅在启用天下一统DLC时可用。

Mandala governments can create new holdings and cadet branches and ask rulers to become tributaries willingly, and unlock the Capital Temple Complex and Capital Temple Complex Upgrade great projects as well as the Khlon Glan and Court Brahmin court positions. They also have access to the Decree law but the only succession available is Mandala Succession. They cannot build or upgrade Temple holdings but can build and upgrade Temple Citadel holdings instead, with it's own line of buildings. Rulers with Mandala government can always designate their heir and it costs  Piety instead of  Prestige. Dynasty Heads with Mandala government also unlock the Divine Aspirations legacy track.

Rulers with Mandala government have 8 levels of  Legitimacy and are affected the same way regardless of their primary title rank. They also inherit the Level of Devotion of their predecessor and can reach the final three Levels of Devotion if their predecessor had the highest Level of Devotion they could reach and died of natural causes or religion and they also unlock the Raze Capital Temple Complex casus belli against each other. Mandala rulers have their  Domain and Vassal Limit capped at 4 and the main way to increase them is by upgrading the Capital Temple Complex.

Every ruler with Mandala government has a Mandala Radiance value, which depends on their Level of Devotion, level of aspect, perks, whether they are independent or not, and number of Capital Temple Complex buildings and decreases with distance from the capital. Mandala Radiance affects the acceptance of other rulers to become tributaries. If the Capital Temple Complex building in the realm capital is ruined, Mandala Radiance will be set to 0.

Every year every Mandala ruler has a 75% chance for a ritual to appear on the map within their borders. The chance is reduced to 25% if the realm is at war. Rituals are optional and performing a ritual will increase  Piety and/or Legitimacy but failing a ritual will reduce them. Rituals come in three tiers, which determine the size of their reward, and the chance for the ritual to be of a higher tier scales with the Popular Opinion of the county where it takes place. Performing a ritual involves traveling to the barony in question (unless it is the realm capital) and making a choice.

Mandala government has different requirements to create titles. Counties held by tributaries are considered owned, although at least one county from the title being created must be held directly. Creating titles costs  Piety instead of  Gold and requires a sufficient Level of Devotion: 500 Piety and Faithful for  Duchy, 2500 Piety and Devoted Servant for  Kingdom and 5000 Piety and Religious Icon for  Empire.

Only rulers with a rite that has the  Polytheist main doctrine can have Mandala government. If they convert to a rite with the  Monotheist main doctrine, their government is changed to  Feudal. In addition, if a Mandala ruler is vassalized by a liege with a different government, they will adopt the liege's government type.

Mandala Aspect

Every house head with Mandala government can choose a Mandala Aspect, which can be upgraded every 5 years up to 4 times once the aspect's upgrade requirements are met. The aspect can be changed at the cost of 500  Piety but this will reset it back to level 1. If the aspect is upgraded to its maximum level, upon succession the heir will receive the option to automatically pass the Mandala Succession trial challenge from the current aspect.

  • Level 2 aspects cost 250  Prestige and 1000  Piety and requires the  Pilgrim trait to have at least 25 experience
  • Level 3 aspects cost 500  Prestige and 2500  Piety and requires the  Pilgrim trait to have at least 50 experience
  • Level 4 aspects cost 1000  Prestige and 5000  Piety and requires the  Pilgrim trait to have at least 75 experience
  • Level 5 aspects cost 2000  Prestige and 10000  Piety and requires the  Pilgrim trait to have maximum experience

瓦努阿

仅在启用天下一统DLC时可用。

瓦努阿政体可以使用部落权威法律，解锁收服与征服宣战理由，并可以建立共同君主制。瓦努阿统治者可以在主要河流中航行，除非其教派禁止，否则可以进行劫掠，并且在赢得战斗时额外获得100 威望。然而，他们无法建造领地，也无法使用部落时代之后的革新，只能使用邦联分割继承法，且初始直辖领上限最高为2。瓦努阿统治者可以使用次等的王廷。拥有瓦努阿政体的王朝首领还会解锁 神圣志向传承路线。

瓦努阿统治者可以使用 易货商品资源，用于支付常备军的维护费。他们还会解锁将其军队组建为易货者的能力，易货者可以前往其他统治者的国家，以2:1的比例将 易货商品兑换为 金币。选中易货者军队后，将鼠标悬停在某个伯爵领上，会显示可获得多少 金币及其 易货商品花费；将鼠标悬停在正在易货的军队上，则会显示兑换完成还需要多长时间。易货的数量取决于军队的规模。易货者军队可能遭到劫掠军队的攻击，若其被击败，劫掠军队将夺走他们的战利品。易货会使该伯爵领获得持续5年的 近期已易货修正，该修正会阻止进一步易货，并提供以下加成：

  • +10% 领地税收
  • -10% 建筑建造时间
  • +0.3 每月发展度
  • 无法被劫掠

发展度仅影响补给上限，且在由瓦努阿统治者统治的伯爵领中不会被动增长。瓦努阿男爵领仅向属于其他政体的军队提供其一半的补给上限。

瓦努阿政体具有以下独特效果：

  • 无论头衔等级如何，瓦努阿统治者的封臣上限均为4。
  • 瓦努阿统治者若未颁布2级部落权威，便无法监禁封臣或廷臣。
  • 瓦努阿封臣提供的税收和征召兵取决于领主的名望等级。
  • −50% Title creation cost.
  • +0.2 monthly  Prestige.

如果未安装该DLC，所有瓦努阿统治者都将使用部落制政体。
