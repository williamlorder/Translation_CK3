# Activity

> License: CC BY-SA 3.0 (Paradox Wikis)

This article has been verified for the current PC [version](https://ck3.paradoxwikis.com/CK3_Wiki:Versioning) (1.20) of the game.


[](https://ck3.paradoxwikis.com/File:Activity_travel.png)  [](https://ck3.paradoxwikis.com/File:Activity_travel.png)Planning a pilgrimage travel

Activities are special occasions hosted by a certain character. Most activities have multiple variants to choose from. Each activity goes through a number of phases and during each phase there is a chance every 7 days for an event to happen. A character can only take part in one activity as a time and characters with the [] Incapable or a disease trait cannot host or join activities.

Most activities requires other characters to join. If not a single character joins the activity will be canceled and the character who started it will gain 20 [] Stress. After an activity started, characters who were not initially invited can be invited manually via a character interaction.

The character starting an activity is the Host and chooses a barony for the activity, with the cost depending on the barony. The map will pin the best 5 baronies for the activity but any valid barony can be selected. All characters who attend an activity must [travel](https://ck3.paradoxwikis.com/Travel) to the designated barony unless it's their realm capital. Each character will travel with an Entourage made up of characters relevant to the activity.


## Cost


The base cost to host an activity depends on host's primary title. It is then modified by various cultural, religious and event modifiers, and culture era. Lastly, costs for activity options, travel options and caravan master are added.

### University Visit


University Visit is an activity available only to adults who do not have a tier 5 education trait and can only be held every 20 years. It can target any barony that has a university, holy building or Ecclesiastical Estate with a fully upgraded [](https://ck3.paradoxwikis.com/File:Domicile_building_church_school.png) Cathedral School building is the capital of China. Constantinople can also be selected and receives a bonus. If the barony's faith considers the character's faith Hostile the activity will cost 200 [] Piety. If the barony has a Confucian faith, culture with Chinese [] Heritage, or is owned by Hegemony of China, completing the activity will give the [] Confucian Education trait or experience for it.

The activity has one Activity Option: Books and Notes. Level 2 costs 150 [] Gold, gives 3 Studiousness and increases a random skill by 1. Level 3 costs 300 [] Gold, gives with 10 Studiousness, increases a random skill by 1, will increase the amount of [] Confucian Education trait experience if the location would give it, and will give a Trinket artifact if the character does not have a Royal Court and a Book artifact if the character has a Royal Court.

University Visits have a meter called *Studiousness*, which ranges from 0 to 100 and decides which rewards the character will receive when the activity ends. The starting amount depends on the level of Books and Notes. If the barony is Constantinople, it will give an additional 10 points. If the barony has a completed [](https://ck3.paradoxwikis.com/File:Great_project_china.png) Strengthen Capital great project, it will give an additional 10 points.

### Examinations


| [](https://ck3.paradoxwikis.com/All_Under_Heaven)
| Available only with the [All Under Heaven](https://ck3.paradoxwikis.com/All_Under_Heaven) DLC enabled.|


Examinations are activities available to adult rulers who have * [Celestial](https://ck3.paradoxwikis.com/Celestial), [] [Meritocratic](https://ck3.paradoxwikis.com/Meritocratic) or [] [Meritocratic Khanate](https://ck3.paradoxwikis.com/Meritocratic_Khanate) government and can only be held in the realm capital. Activity guests can gain the [] [Confucian Education](https://ck3.paradoxwikis.com/Traits#Confucian_Education) trait or experience for it but the activity host cannot. Every character who takes an examination has an Exam Score*, which determines their chance to pass the examination. The initial Exam Score depends on the experience in the [] Confucian Education trait, [] Martial, [] Stewardship, [] Learning, whether they know the host's language, and whether they've been caught cheating before or not. The activity host will have the option to increase the Exam Score of certain characters depending on the activity intent. Participating in examinations is required for a character's [Merit Rank](https://ck3.paradoxwikis.com/Attributes#Merit_Rank) to rise beyond a certain point.

Only landless characters can take an examination. If a governor attempts to attend an activity and didn't pass all their examinations it will receive the option to abdicate.

Characters who take an examination can cheat but this has a 90% chance to give a secret and a 10% chance to get caught. If the character is caught it will have the following options to get the secret instead:

- Pay 100 [] Influence.

- Attempt an [] Intrigue challenge. If the challenge is failed the character will gain **+40** [] Stress.

- If a [] Friend, [] Best Friend or [](https://ck3.paradoxwikis.com/File:Lover.png) Lover is present in the same activity they can be blamed but the relation will change to [] Rival.


If none of these options are taken the character will fail the examination, gain **-350** [] Prestige, **-60** [] [Merit](https://ck3.paradoxwikis.com/Merit) and a [] Stress Level, and for the rest of their life they will have reduced Exam Score and certain event choices during examinations will become unavailable.

If no examination activity has been held in Hegemony of China for the last 6 years, the [Dynastic Cycle](https://ck3.paradoxwikis.com/Dynastic_Cycle) situation will be pushed towards the [](https://ck3.paradoxwikis.com/File:Dynastic_cycle_phase_instability.png) Tension era each year. The effect is doubled if no examination activity has been held for the last 10 years.

### Funeral


| [](https://ck3.paradoxwikis.com/Legends_of_the_Dead)
| Available only with the [Legends of the Dead](https://ck3.paradoxwikis.com/Legends_of_the_Dead) DLC enabled.|


Funeral is an activity available only to rulers who have any dead relative they didn't use the activity on and can only be held every 5 years in any realm barony. It has a single activity option, Ceremonials, which affects the reward. When the activity starts it will give 25 [] Prestige. When the activity ends it will give the following rewards:

- **+20** [] Legitimacy, **+200** [] Piety and **-20** [] Stress if Ceremonials is at level 1

- **+50** [] Legitimacy, **+250** [] Piety and **-35** [] Stress if Ceremonials is at level 2

- **+100** [] Legitimacy, **+350** [] Piety and **-60** [] Stress if Ceremonials is at level 3

- **+10** [] Legitimacy and **+50** [] Piety if the barony has a [](https://ck3.paradoxwikis.com/File:Holding_church.png) Temple holding and the same faith

- **+10** [] Legitimacy and **+50** [] Piety if the barony has a holy building

- **+10** [] Legitimacy and **+50** [] Piety if the barony is a holy site

- **+10** [] Legitimacy and **+50** [] Piety if the barony has a [](https://ck3.paradoxwikis.com/File:Building_tower_of_silence.png) Great Tower of Silence building

- **+10** [] Legitimacy and **+50** [] Piety if the barony has [](https://ck3.paradoxwikis.com/Mountains) Mountains or [](https://ck3.paradoxwikis.com/Desert_Mountains) Desert Mountains terrain and rite has the Sky Burials doctrine

- [] Piety rewards are reduced by **-90%** if rite has the Mummification doctrine and increased by **+25%** if rite has the Stoic Burial doctrine

- [] Stress rewards are reduced by **-50%** if rite has the Stoic Burial doctrine and increased by **+200%** if rite has the Bewailing Burial doctrine


If the rite has the Mummification doctrine, the host will receive the choice between gaining an artifact or an additional 250 [] Piety. The rarity of the artifact depends on the traits (especially if they are virtues or sins) and title rank of the deceased as well as whether they were a head of faith. Every ruler can choose the artifact to be a Trinket and rulers who have a Royal Court can choose the artifact to be Furniture instead.


### Camp Revelry


| [](https://ck3.paradoxwikis.com/Roads_to_Power)
| Available only with the [Roads to Power](https://ck3.paradoxwikis.com/Roads_to_Power) DLC enabled.|


Camp Revelry is an activity available only to adventurers which lasts 3 months. It has a base cost of 15 [] Gold and two activity options:

- [](https://ck3.paradoxwikis.com/File:Feast_option_food.png) Food determines the starting Carousing. Increasing it to level 2 or 3 requires a Supply Tent domicile building and can trigger events to pay [] Provisions and gain a either [] Piety or a follower.

- [](https://ck3.paradoxwikis.com/File:Tour_option_luxuries.png) Drinks give determine whether the guests will gain [] Opinion with the host how much experience the [] Eager Reveler trait will give. Level 2 requires a Supply Tent domicile building. Level 3 requires the [](https://ck3.paradoxwikis.com/File:Domicile_building_brewers.png) Mobile Brewers upgrade and gives the Master of Ceremonies modifier for 3 years at the end of the activity.


The ruler of the barony where the camp is located can be invited as a guest of honor. The chance for them to accept decreases with their primary title rank.

Camp Revelries have a meter called Carousing, which ranges from 0 to 100 and decides which rewards the host will receive when the activity ends.

- Sacrificing majestic animals costs scaled [] Gold and gives 100 [] Piety and 30 [] Opinion for 5 years with every character of the same faith in the realm.

- Sacrificing humans is free and gives 100 [] Piety and 30 [] Opinion for 5 years with every character of the same faith in the realm but characters and counties whose faith does not have the Gruesome Festivals or Human Sacrifice tenets will lose 50 [] Opinion for 5 years.

- Sacrificing majestic humans costs scaled [] Gold and gives 250 [] Piety and 30 [] Opinion for 5 years with every character of the same faith in the realm but characters and counties whose faith does not have the Gruesome Festivals or Human Sacrifice tenets will lose 50 [] Opinion.

This can only happen once per faith.

If the rite has the Ritual Cannibalism core tenet and human sacrifice was selected, every noble character who attends the activity will gain the [] Cannibal trait if it doesn't have it already.

If the rite has a [] Temporal head of faith, the host will have the option to gain 50 [] Opinion with them instead of the bonuses from Festival Size.

#### Contests


A Grand Tournament is made up of between 1 and 5 Contests. Each character can choose whether to take part in a contest or merely observe. Taking part in a Contest grants the following rewards:

- Every character who qualified will gain the [](https://ck3.paradoxwikis.com/File:Trait_tournament.png) Hastiluder trait or experience for it and gain a positive modifier for 5 years.

- The character in the 3rd place gains a small amount of [] Prestige. If they are an [] Accolade and the tournament had 4 or 5 contests their liege will gain a tiny amount of [] Prestige as well.

- The character in the 2nd place gains a moderate amount of [] Prestige. If they are an [] Accolade their liege will gain a small amount of [] Prestige as well.

- The character in the 1st place gains a large amount of [] Prestige, an artifact, and a stronger positive modifier for 5 years. If they are an [] Accolade their liege will gain a moderate amount of [] Prestige as well.

- If the host spent at least 180 [] Gold on Prizes then the contestants in the first 3 places will also receive a scaled amount of [] Gold.If they are an [] Accolade they will give some or all the [] Gold to their liege depending on their [] Opinion of the liege.


Characters will have a Progress to Victory bar showing how close they are to winning the current Contest. At least 20 progress is required to qualify for a Contest. If the host fails to qualify but a rival does not, the host will have the option to replace the the rival in the contest. This will give **-20** [] Opinion with the rival.
