# Doctrines

> Source: https://ck3.paradoxwikis.com/Doctrines
> License: CC BY-SA 3.0 (Paradox Wikis)

Each rite has a large number of **doctrines**, which detail what is allowed and what is forbidden within a rite. They also affect how AI characters with the  Zealous trait will act. Doctrines that a character's faith doesn't know can be learned from events or the [](/File:Icon_scheme_study_faith.png) Study Rite scheme.
## Main doctrines
Main doctrines determine the rules of the faith itself and have a considerable impact on how the realm is governed.

## Marriage doctrines
Marriage doctrines determine who a character can marry, when can they marry or divorce, and the status of children born out of formal relationships.

## Crime doctrines
Crime doctrines determine whether certain traits are accepted, shunned, crimes or virtues.
- If the trait is a crime then characters will keep it secret. If discovered, the character can be blackmailed for a  Strong Hook. If the trait is exposed the character will lose  Opinion with all characters whose rite has the doctrine and they'll be considered a criminal.
- If the trait is a shunned then characters will keep it secret. If discovered, the character can be blackmailed for a  Weak Hook. If the trait is exposed the character will lose  Opinion with all characters whose rite has the doctrine.
- If the trait is accepted then characters will be open about it. If the character is a vassal or courtier then their liege's faith must also consider it accepted or virtuous for this to happen.
- If the trait is a virtuous then characters will be open about it and the trait will be considered a  Virtue with a Virtue Weight of 200. If the character is a vassal or courtier then their liege's faith must also consider it accepted or virtuous for this to happen.
> The tables in this section contained in-game flavor text (official text not covered by the wiki's CC BY-SA licence) and have been omitted. See the original page: https://ck3.paradoxwikis.com/Doctrines

## Clergy doctrines
Clergy doctrines have various effects that only apply to [court chaplains](/Court_Chaplain), [realm priests](/Realm_Priest), temple holders, and characters with the  Monk trait.
> The tables in this section contained in-game flavor text (official text not covered by the wiki's CC BY-SA licence) and have been omitted. See the original page: https://ck3.paradoxwikis.com/Doctrines

### Coronation Tradition
The Coronation Tradition doctrine decides who can use what activity variant when planning a [](/File:Activity_coronation.png) Coronation activity. Changing the doctrine when creating a new rite has a base cost of 400  Piety and does not cause  Divergence regardless of the current faith's doctrines.

## Special doctrines
Special doctrines are encountered on some faiths as an additional doctrine. They are inherited when a character following one of those faiths creates a new rite.
