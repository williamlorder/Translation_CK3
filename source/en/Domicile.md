Domiciles are available to characters whose [government](/Government) type goes not require them to own landed titles. The appearance of buildings in a domicile depends on the character's culture and the terrain depends on the terrain of the [barony](/Barony) where the domicile is located. There are five types of domiciles:
- [](/File:Adventurer.png) [Adventurer](/Adventurer) governments have a [Camp](/Camp)
- Ecclesiastical governments have an [Ecclesiastical estate](/Ecclesiastical_estate)
- [Administrative](/Administrative) governments have an [Administrative estate](/Administrative_estate)
- [Celestial](/Celestial),  [Meritocratic](/Meritocratic) and  [Meritocratic Khanate](/Meritocratic_Khanate) governments have a [Celestial estate](/Celestial_estate)
- [Ritsuryō](/Ritsury%C5%8D) and  [Sōryō](/S%C5%8Dry%C5%8D) governments have a [Manor](/Manor)
- [Nomadic](/Nomadic) governments have a [Nomadic capital](/Nomadic_capital)
