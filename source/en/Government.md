A government is the system that rules [characters](/Character) and their [titles](/Titles). It determines the [laws](/Laws) available to a character as well as any obligations (taxes and levies) a [vassal](/Subjects) may have to their liege. Governments can only be changed via decisions. Governments are divided into three types:
- **Landed governments** require the character to own landed titles. If a player character loses all their landed titles, they must choose to play as another dynasty member or receive a game over but if the [](/Roads_to_Power) Roads to Power DLC is installed, they will have the option to choose between doing that or changing government to [Adventurer](/Adventurer). If a character with a landed government inherits a realm of higher rank they will adopt the realm's government.
- **Domicile governments** give their house heads or potentially all rulers a [domicile](/Domicile), which allows a player to play as them even if they do not own landed titles. If a ruler changes their government to a landed one, their domicile is sold for  [Gold](/Resources#Gold), all title men-at-arms are removed, and if their government used  [Treasury](/Resources#Treasury) or  [Herd](/Resources#Herd) they are converted to  Gold as well. If a character with a domicile government inherits a realm of higher rank they will not change government.
- **Non-dynastic governments** do not use dynastic succession and as a result are not playable. When a character with a non-dynastic government dies another random character will inherit all titles. If a character with a non-dynastic government inherits a realm of higher rank they will not change government.
## Landed governments
### Tribal
Tribal governments have access to the Tribal Authority law, unlock Subjugate and Conquest casus belli, and can start a Co-Monarchy. All tribal rulers can raid unless their rite forbids it and gain 100 more  Prestige when they win a battle. However, they cannot construct holdings or use innovations beyond the Tribal Era. Tribal rulers can only use the Confederate Partition succession law but unlock the Challenge the Ruler decision. Tribal rulers have access to an inferior [Royal Court](/Royal_court).
[Development](/Development) only affects the Supply Limit and does not passively grow in counties ruled by a tribal ruler. Tribal baronies only provide half of their supply limit to armies belonging to other governments.
Tribal governments have the following unique effects:
- Tribal rulers pay for [men-at-arms](/Men-at-arms) using  Prestige instead of  Gold but the maintenance cost is reduced by 30%.
- Tribal rulers cannot [imprison](/Prisoner) vassals or [courtiers](/Courtiers) without passing level 2 Tribal Authority.
- Tribal vassals provide taxes and levies based on the Liege's [Level of Fame](/Resources#Fame).
- **−50%**  [Title](/Titles) creation cost.
- **+0.2** monthly  Prestige.
- **−20**  Opinion with vassals that do not have  Tribal,  Wanua or  Nomadic government
### Feudal
Feudal governments can create new holdings and cadet branches, have access to the Crown Authority law, can apply Title Succession laws and can start a Co-Monarchy.
### Clan
Clan governments can create new holdings and cadet branches and have access to the Crown Authority law. All powerful vassals will want to be in an Alliance with their liege and will gain **-30**  Opinion of their liege if they are not. When clan rulers are at peace, they can appoint a Vizier. House heads with this government also unlock interactions to give house members the  Extolled by House or  Accused of Decadence traits.
If a clan ruler is a Dynasty Head, their realm will be named after their dynasty rather than the primary title. This behavior can be disabled in game settings.
#### Tax Jurisdictions
[](/File:Tax_Jurisdictions.png)  [](/File:Tax_Jurisdictions.png)Tax Jurisdictions
Vassals with Clan government provide taxes and levies via Tax Jurisdictions. If a ruler has vassals with Clan government then the Tax Jurisdictions tab will become available on the sidebar. Each Tax Jurisdiction is made up of a  Tax Collector and up to 12 Taxpayers. Taxpayers are assigned automatically but can be manually added or removed afterwards. Count and Duke rulers can appoint 1  Tax Collector while King, Emperor and Hegemon rulers can appoint 2. Cultures with the Enlightened Magnates or Legalistic tradition can each appoint an additional one, as can dynasties with the first [](/File:Khvarenah_legacy_track.png) Brilliance legacy. A Vizier, depending on its Stewardship score, can grant between 1 and 5 additional  Tax Collector. Two Innovations, Magistrates and Court Officials, also each grant an additional one. First perk from Avaricious stewardship lifestyle tree - Golden obligation also grants one 1  Tax Collector. Only unlanded courtiers can be appointed as  Tax Collector and only if the rite's View on Gender doctrine allows it. How much taxes and levies a Tax Jurisdiction provides depends on the  Tax Collector's aptitude, which is the following:
- **+1** per  Diplomacy (max 50)
- **+1** per  Martial (max 50)
- **+1** per  Stewardship (max 50)
- **+1** per  Intrigue (max 50)
- **+2** per  Learning (max 50)
- **+5** per 20  Opinion
- **+10** if  Just
- **+10** if  Administrator
- **+10** if  Avaricious
- **+10** if  Diplomat
- **+15** if culture has the Enlightened Magnates tradition
- **+20** if culture has the Family Business tradition
- **-10** if vassal

#### House unity
[](/File:Impassive_house_unity.png)  [](/File:Impassive_house_unity.png)Impassive house unity
If a House Head has  Clan government, then its house will have an additional mechanic called House Unity, representing how well members of a house tend to get along with each other. It will be displayed for all house members but only those who have Clan government will be affected by its effects. House Unity is increased by taking Unifying Actions towards them and decreased by taking Divisive Actions towards them. House heads also unlock the Steer House Unity decision.
The House Head also gains access to interactions to give and remove from house members the  Extolled by House or  Accused of Decadence trait. Both interactions cost  Piety that scales with how many virtue and sin traits the targeted house member has. Each trait can only be given once per 3 years. If a character with one of these traits becomes House Head the trait is lost.

### Theocratic
Theocratic governments are used by clergy members of rites that have the  Theocratic clerical tradition and their religion is not  Christianity. Theocratic governments can appear in every game but are only playable if the [](/By_God_Alone) By God Alone DLC is installed.
Theocratic governments use the succession law with the same name and the Church Authority law, can hold  Temple holdings, do not use  Legitimacy, do not have access to Coronation laws, and their [](/File:Bishop_job.png) Realm Priest provide all their taxes and levies regardless of  Opinion.
Theocratic governments also do not have access to casus belli except Independence and Holy War but heads of rite whose level of devotion is at least  Faithful unlock the Incite Claim War and Incite Holy War interactions, which cost 100  Piety per title rank of the targeted character. The potential attacker must accept it and acceptance can be increased by offering to join the war, pay scaled  Gold, spend additional  Piety, or use a hook.
If their liege follows a different faith, Theocratic vassals provide their liege with 10% of their  Taxes  Levies. If the liege follows the same faith the provided taxes and levies depend on the liege's level of devotion.
### Mandala
Mandala governments can create new holdings and cadet branches and ask rulers to become tributaries willingly, and unlock the [Capital Temple Complex](/Great_projects#Capital_Temple_Complex) and *Capital Temple Complex Upgrade* great projects as well as the [](/File:Khlon_glan_court_position.png) [Khlon Glan](/Court#Khlon_Glan) and [](/File:Court_brahmin_court_position.png) [Court Brahmin](/Court#Court_Brahmin) court positions. They also have access to the [Decree](/Authority_laws#Decree) law but the only succession available is [Mandala Succession](/Succession_laws#Mandala_succession). They cannot build or upgrade [](/File:Holding_church.png) Temple holdings but can build and upgrade [](/File:Holding_temple_citadel.png) [Temple Citadel](/Barony#Temple_Citadel) holdings instead, with it's [own line of buildings](/Temple_Citadel_buildings). Rulers with Mandala government can always designate their heir and it costs  Piety instead of  Prestige. Dynasty Heads with Mandala government also unlock the [](/File:Southeast_asia_legacy_track.png) [Divine Aspirations](/Dynasty#Divine_Aspirations) legacy track.
Rulers with Mandala government have 8 levels of  [Legitimacy](/Attributes#Mandala_legitimacy) and are affected the same way regardless of their primary title rank. They also inherit the Level of Devotion of their predecessor and can reach the final three Levels of Devotion if their predecessor had the highest Level of Devotion they could reach and died of natural causes or religion and they also unlock the *Raze Capital Temple Complex* casus belli against each other. Mandala rulers have their  Domain and Vassal Limit capped at 4 and the main way to increase them is by upgrading the Capital Temple Complex.
Every ruler with Mandala government has a [Mandala Radiance](/Attributes#Mandala_Radiance) value, which depends on their Level of Devotion, level of aspect, perks, whether they are independent or not, and number of Capital Temple Complex buildings and decreases with distance from the capital. Mandala Radiance affects the acceptance of other rulers to become tributaries. If the Capital Temple Complex building in the realm capital is ruined, Mandala Radiance will be set to 0.
Every year every Mandala ruler has a 75% chance for a ritual to appear on the map within their borders. The chance is reduced to 25% if the realm is at war. Rituals are optional and performing a ritual will increase  Piety and/or [](/File:Mandala_legitimacy_5.png) Legitimacy but failing a ritual will reduce them. Rituals come in three tiers, which determine the size of their reward, and the chance for the ritual to be of a higher tier scales with the Popular Opinion of the county where it takes place. Performing a ritual involves traveling to the barony in question (unless it is the realm capital) and making a choice.
Mandala government has different requirements to create titles. Counties held by tributaries are considered owned, although at least one county from the title being created must be held directly. Creating titles costs  Piety instead of  Gold and requires a sufficient Level of Devotion: 500 Piety and Faithful for  Duchy, 2500 Piety and Devoted Servant for  Kingdom and 5000 Piety and Religious Icon for  Empire.
Only rulers with a rite that has the  [Polytheist](/Doctrines#Theism) main doctrine can have Mandala government. If they convert to a rite with the  Monotheist main doctrine, their government is changed to  Feudal. In addition, if a Mandala ruler is vassalized by a liege with a different government, they will adopt the liege's government type.
#### Mandala Aspect
Every house head with Mandala government can choose a **Mandala Aspect**, which can be upgraded every 5 years up to 4 times once the aspect's upgrade requirements are met. The aspect can be changed at the cost of 500  Piety but this will reset it back to level 1. If the aspect is upgraded to its maximum level, upon succession the heir will receive the option to automatically pass the Mandala Succession trial challenge from the current aspect.
- Level 2 aspects cost 250  Prestige and 1000  Piety and requires the  Pilgrim trait to have at least 25 experience
- Level 3 aspects cost 500  Prestige and 2500  Piety and requires the  Pilgrim trait to have at least 50 experience
- Level 4 aspects cost 1000  Prestige and 5000  Piety and requires the  Pilgrim trait to have at least 75 experience
- Level 5 aspects cost 2000  Prestige and 10000  Piety and requires the  Pilgrim trait to have maximum experience

### Wanua
Wanua governments have access to the Tribal Authority law, unlock Subjugate and Conquest casus belli, and can start a Co-Monarchy. Wanua rulers can sail in major rivers, can raid unless their rite forbids it, and gain 100 more  Prestige when they win a battle. However they cannot construct holdings or use innovations beyond the Tribal Era, can only use the Confederate Partition succession law, and have a starting maximum domain limit of 2. Wanua rulers have access to an inferior [Royal Court](/Royal_court). Dynasty Heads with Wanua government also unlock the [](/File:Southeast_asia_legacy_track.png) Divine Aspirations legacy track.
Wanua rulers gain access to the  Barter Goods resource, which is used to pay for men-at-arms maintenance. They also unlock the ability to raise their armies as Barterers, which can travel to other rulers' countries to convert  Barter Goods into  Gold at a 2:1 ratio. Hovering over a county while a Barterer army is selected will display how much  Gold will be obtained and its cost in  Barter Goods and hovering over an army that is bartering will display how long will it take for the conversion to be finished. How much will be bartered depends on the size of the army. Barterer armies can be attacked by Raiding armies and if they are defeated the Raiding army will take their Loot. Bartering will give the county the [](/File:Modifier_goods_positive.png) Recently Bartered modifier for 5 years, which prevents further bartering and gives the following bonuses:
- **+10%** Holding Taxes
- **-10%** Building Construction Time
- **+0.3** Monthly Development
- Cannot be raided
[Development](/Development) only affects the Supply Limit and does not passively grow in counties ruled by a wanua ruler. Wanua baronies only provide half of their supply limit to armies belonging to other governments.
Wanua governments have the following unique effects:
- Wanua rulers have a Vassal Limit of 4 regardless of title rank.
- Wanua rulers cannot [imprison](/Prisoner) vassals or [courtiers](/Courtiers) without passing level 2 Tribal Authority.
- Wanua vassals provide taxes and levies based on the Liege's [Level of Fame](/Resources#Fame).
- **−50%**  [Title](/Titles) creation cost.
- **+0.2** monthly  Prestige.
If the DLC is not installed, all Wanua rulers will use Tribal government.
