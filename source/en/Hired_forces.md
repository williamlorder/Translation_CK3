# Hired forces

> Source: https://ck3.paradoxwikis.com/Hired_forces
> License: CC BY-SA 3.0 (Paradox Wikis)

Hired forces are predefined [armies](/Army) which may be temporarily controlled by hiring them with  Gold. Hired forces cannot be used to [raid](/Raid). There are two types of hired forces, each one with its own tab in the Military menu.
## Mercenaries
[](/File:Mercenaries_screen.png)  [](/File:Mercenaries_screen.png)The mercenaries screen.
Mercenaries are armies that can be hired by rulers for 3 years. The gold cost is paid upon hiring the Mercenaries and there is no additional cost associated with hiring them, but at the end of the 3 years, they will leave if their contract is not extended. Extending a contract for 3 years has the same cost as initially hiring the army.
Each [culture](/Culture) generates between one and three mercenary companies depending on the number of [counties](/County) of that culture, with each additional company being bigger and more expensive than the previous one. They will pick a county of their culture as their headquarters and will be available to be hired by anyone with [capital](/Capital) within [diplomatic range](/Diplomatic_range) of that county. Range between  Mercenary armies come with one or more specific [men-at-arms](/Men-at-arms) regiments and will also contain a number of [knights](/Knight). Mercenary regiments reinforce 3 times faster than regular regiments. Character can hire mercenaries of their own culture from further away.
Rulers are allowed to go into [debt](/Debt) up to 2 years in order to hire mercenaries.
The cost of hiring mercenaries can be reduced by the following:
- 30% with the Soldiers of Lesser Fortune perk of the Overseer tree.
- 15% with the Communal court type.
- 15% with the "Swords for hire" Tradition, for same culture only.
- < 30% from the "Scoundrel" accolade stat
- 25% from the "Condottieri" regional innovation, for same culture only.
- 50% from "Malleable" subjects Tradition, for same culture only
- Many more...

## Military orders
Military orders are independent military organizations that work to defend and expand the influence of their faith. Each military order has a  Duke rank title and [](/File:Holy_order_government.png) Military Order government.
Holdings leased to a military order may be revoked by any ruler in de jure hierarchy whose realm contains the leased holding and is at peace unless the grandmaster has a  Strong Hook on them. Revoking the last leased holding of a military order will destroy it. Revoking the lease of a military order gives **-50**  Opinion with the grandmaster and **-30**  Opinion with the military order's head of faith. It will also give the following:
- **-1**  Development in the county if leased holding was level 1 or 2
- **-1**  Development in the county if leased holding was level 3 or 4
- **-5**  Spiritual Fulfillment if the military order belongs to a faith considered Righteous, Astray or same faith
- **-500**  Piety if the military order belongs to a faith considered Righteous or same faith
- **-100**  Piety if the military order belongs to a faith considered Astray
- **+250**  Piety and **+20**  Opinion with the head of faith if the military order belongs to a faith considered Hostile or Evil but the military order's faith will also gain 10  Fervor
- If the barony's county had the same faith as the military order it will gain **-15** Popular Opinion and **-0.15** Monthly Control for 10 years
Rulers of a military order's faith may hire them if they are at war with a faith considered Hostile or Evil, regardless of casus belli. A ruler may only have one military order hired at a time. Hiring military orders costs  Piety unless the ruler is the order's Patron, scaling on the maximum amount of levies and regiments the military order has. Military orders remain hired for as long as their employer is at war with someone of another faith.
Military orders have 500  Levies, 2  Knights and 2 regiments. Each holding they own provides an additional regiment and 200  Levies and every two holdings they own provide an additional  Knight. Their Men-at-Arms do not gain bonuses from the buildings in its holdings, but rather from their employer's buildings.
### Creating military orders
Creating a military order costs 1000  Piety and 500  Gold or  Treasury and has the following requirements:
- Adult
- At peace
- King or above
- Title rank requirement is reduced to  Duke if the ruler is involved in the Iberian Struggle and the struggle phase is [](/File:Struggle_iberia_phase_hostility.png) Hostility
- Devoted Servant or higher level of devotion
- Not already the Patron of a military order
- Rite does not have the [](/File:Special_doctrine_immaterial_harmony.png) Immaterial Harmony special doctrine
- If the faith has a head of faith rulers must also have positive  Opinion with it to create a military order
- If the [](/By_God_Alone) By God Alone DLC is installed and faith has the  Theocratic clerical tradition then it also requires the character to have  Ecclesiastical or [](/File:Theocracy_government.png) Theocratic government, a [](/File:External_theological_agent_puppet.png) Clerical Ruler Puppet or a [](/File:Theological_agent_puppet.png) Realm Priest Puppet. Creating a military order costs 1000  Piety and 500  Gold or  Treasury.
Creating a military order requires an owned  Castle or  City holding that aren't a county capital to be given as the order's headquarters.
When a military order is created the ruler who created it will become its Patron and the order's grandmaster will belong to its rite and culture. Its faith will gain **+5**  Fervor and if it has a head of faith they will gain **+30**  Opinion for 20 years with the ruler who created the military order. In addition the ruler who created the military order will gain **+0.5** Piety per month and **+10**  Opinion with every character of the same faith for 5 years.
AI rulers will never create military orders if the faith already has 5 or more.
### Patronage
A  King or above who controls the barony where a military order is located will be its patron, even if they are a subject. If the top liege is a  Duke or below the military order will have no patron. Patrons of a military order may hire them at no cost, as long as they belong to the same faith. They may also get events that allows them to grant holdings to the new military order.
If a military order is hired by another character, its Patron can repay their employer the  Piety hiring cost to hire the military order. This option is not available in multiplayer if the military order is hired by another human player.
### Members
All members of a military order gain the  Military Order Member trait. Rulers of the same faith can ask the courtiers who have the same faith, a  Martial education trait, are not  Excommunicated or diarch and are allowed by gender laws to join the military order. If the faith has multiple military orders the ruler will be asked which military order to pick. The courtier must accept the offer, with its Boldness and Zeal playing a major effect. They will always refuse if they have the  Ambitious or  Pregnant trait.
Sending a courtier to a military order costs 100  Piety. If the courtier is a close family member the cost is increased to 200  Piety. If they are the Primary Heir the cost is increased to 350  Piety and 50  Legitimacy.
