# Holy sites

> Source: https://ck3.paradoxwikis.com/Holy_sites
> License: CC BY-SA 3.0 (Paradox Wikis)

This article has been verified for the current PC version (1.20) of the game.

Holy sites are baronies that are considered of religious significance by a faith and provide bonuses if held by a character of their faith. Most faiths start with 5 holy sites and can have a maximum of 9. When reforming or creating a new faith, the new faith will inherit the holy sites of the old faith. Rulers can create, remove and change the status of holy sites in their domain if they meet one of the following conditions:

- If faith has a head of faith the ruler have them as a puppet
- If faith does not have a head of faith the ruler must have the Paragon of Virtue level of devotion or above and either a  Realm Priest Puppet,  Ecclesiastical government or  Theocratic government
- Heads of faith can also remove and change the status of holy sites of their faith and do not need them in domain to do so.
New holy sites cannot be created in baronies that another faith considers a holy site but these holy sites can be adopted instead at the cost of 1000  Piety. The holy site much belong to the same religion unless the rite has the syncretism core tenet matching the religion of the holy site's faith or has the Adaptability or Adoptionism tenet and is the mainline rite. If a barony with a special building is a holy site for a faith then all religion restrictions on its special building are removed.

Every holy site can have up to 3 holy relics that require the same faith enshrined or removed either on creation or later by the title holder, top liege, or the owner of the clerical region where the holy site is located. Enshrining and removing holy relics increase or reduce some of the bonuses the holy site gives. Enshrining a holy relic gives +15  Opinion with every  Zealot and  Minority vassal as well as the head of faith for 10 years. Removing a holy relic gives -20  Opinion with every  Zealot and  Minority vassal as well as the head of faith for 10 years and it also costs 100-500  Piety and Devotion depending on the rarity of the removed holy relic. Holy relics can also be swapped but if the new holy relic is of lower rarity this will still give the penalties. If the last enshrined holy relic is removed the holy site will become inactive until another holy relic is enshrined. If a holy site it abandoned all enshrined relics will be given to the ruler who holds the country with the former holy site barony.

Creating a holy sits costs 2000  Piety and requires a total artifact rarity of at least 3 among all enshrined holy relics. Removing a holy site costs 1000  Piety, gives -20  Opinion with every  Zealot and  Parochial vassal of the same faith for 10 years, and if the holy site had an owner it also gives -50  Opinion with them for 10 years.

## Holy site status

Holy sites have one of two statuses. Changing the status of a holy site costs 1000  Piety and can only be done every 5 years.

- Regular holy sites provide a bonus to their holder, a bonus to their county that scales with holy relic rarity, can be used for  Pilgrimage activities by any character who follows the same faith, allow the construction of a holy building by any ruler in de jure hierarchy who follows the same faith, and are necessary to create a Head of Faith title or to reform an  Unreformed faith. Turning an eminent holy site into a regular holy site gives -20  Opinion with every  Zealot and  Parochial vassal of the same faith for 10 years and if the holy site had an owner it also gives -50  Opinion with them for 10 years.
- Eminent holy sites provide all the bonuses of regular holy sites and also provide a bonus to all character of their faith. Each faith can only have 3 eminent holy sites. Turning a regular holy site into an eminent holy site gives +30  Opinion with every  Zealot vassal of the same faith for 10 years and if the holy site had an owner it also gives +50  Opinion with them for 10 years.
