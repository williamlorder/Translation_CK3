  Starting innovations for the Greek culture
**Innovations** represent technological, legal and ideological advances that a [Culture](https://ck3.paradoxwikis.com/Culture) can make. Once an innovation is unlocked, it can be used by any County and Character of the culture which unlocked it. Innovations are grouped into 4 categories: Military and Civic, Cultural, and Regional. The [[DLC All Under Heaven.png]](https://ck3.paradoxwikis.com/All_Under_Heaven) All Under Heaven DLC adds a fifth category - Silk Road.

- Military and Civic innovations have a small chance to progress towards being unlocked each month based on multiple factors, the most important being average Development in Counties of the Culture. The second most important factor is the number of neighbor Counties that belong to another Culture which already unlocked an innovation (Exposure). The advancement chance is 5% + Fascination % + 40% if the innovation is the current Culture Spread. Being fascinated with the same innovation as the Culture Spread innovation also adds an additional advancement chance of up to 200% at 100% Cultural Acceptance.

- The Culture Head of each culture can choose one innovation at a time to be that culture's Fascination.  The Fascination bonus is 10% as a base, plus an additional 1% per the Culture Head's skill that matches the innovation from 1-10, then 0.9% for 11-20, 0.8% for 21-30, et cetera. The maximum bonus from the Culture Head's skill including the base is 45%, then Cultural Fascination modifiers are applied afterwards. For instance, a Culture Head with 15 Stewardship and the Bureaucratic Culture (+5% Cultural Fascination Progress) who is fascinated with the Coinage innovation will receive a fascination bonus of = ( 10% + [ 1% * 10 ] + [ 0.9% * 5 ] ) * ( 1 + 5% ) = 25.725%.

- Each culture will also have Cultural Spread from another nearby culture if they have an undiscovered innovation, focusing on one innovation at a time similar to Fascination. Cultures with the highest cultural acceptance are prioritized, but which innovation spread from them cannot be chosen. The Cultural Spread bonus is 40%.

- Regional innovations are unlocked when the Culture has a sufficient presence in a certain Region, and remain unlocked even if said territory is lost.

- Cultural innovations are always active, but require a certain culture or tradition.

- Silk Road innovations are initially available only to Han culture. After Han culture researches them, cultures with Chinese heritage, the Sinophilic tradition or a ruler who controls a hub in a Silk Road situation region with the innovation can research these innovations as well. Other rulers must unlock them by taking the Visit a Silk Road Market decision and passing the skill challenge.

Amount of advancement gained = 0.3 + (average cultural province Development * 0.02) + 0.2 if ahead in era.

Random Innovations by decisions like [Unite the Slavs](https://ck3.paradoxwikis.com/Unite_the_Slavs) are decided by the in-game date.

## Eras

Each Culture will be within one of 4 Eras which determine which innovations the Culture can unlock. Once at least 8 innovations from an Era are unlocked and the required minimum year is reached, the Culture will slowly progress to the next Era based on the average  Development in the Culture's counties. If at least 8 innovations are unlocked, innovations from the next era can be selected by Culture Heads as Fascination even if the era is not reached but the progress will be much slower.

Rulers with  Tribal,  Nomadic or  Wanua government cannot use innovations later than Tribal Era ones. If a Culture Head has such a government its culture cannot progress to another Era and if it's already in another era progress towards unlocking the Fascination will be much slower.
