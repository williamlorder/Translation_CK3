Situations are major historical incidents that involve more than one ruler and are happening over a period of time. Situations go through multiple phases, which determine how each ruler is affected by them.


## Natural disasters


Natural disasters cannot happen in the first 10 years of a game. Afterwards, one of each natural disaster will take place every year. Selecting a disaster will display all affected counties with a flashing red border. Natural disasters are 2 times less likely to take place in the de jure capital of a  Duchy, and 4 times less likely if it's also the de jure capital of a  Kingdom. They are also 10 times less likely to take place in a region that already had a natural disaster.


Natural disaster situations affect the owners of counties where the disaster takes place, as well as any liege they have.


Every natural disaster has one of three levels of severity.


Every natural disaster situation has 3 phases.


- Warning phases last 40 days; halfway through, every affected ruler will receive the option to gain 10  Stress and enter a regency while the situation lasts so that recovery contributions will cost 10% less. If the ruler is already in a regency the ruler will not gain  Stress.

- Impact phases last 30 days. Every affected ruler will lose 50  Legitimacy if not Hegemon of China and 100  Legitimacy if Hegemon of China and every location will lose  Control, will no longer increase in  Development, and will receive a modifier based on the situation's severity for 25 years. Every 7 days characters will die, every army located in the situation region has a 25-75% chance to be damaged and lose supplies (depending on its size), and the rulers affected will receive a random negative event. The map terrain will visibly change until the situation ends.

- Recovery phases last for 15 years and give every affected ruler -0.25  Monthly Legitimacy. Affected rulers can pay various contributions to receive bonuses. The cost of each contribution depends on the number of counties affected, the severity of the disaster, the ruler's  Tax income and the innovations from their culture. If all mandatory contributions have been paid, the situation will end after another two months, and all penalties from the disaster will be removed. If the disaster affects multiple independent realms, each realm must pay the contributions separately to end the disaster within their own realm.

If a human player would be picked for death during a situation, both the player's character and a close family member, always the primary heir if there is one, will be at risk and the player will receive the following choices:


- Try to survive together through a twice as difficult  Prowess challenge. Both characters will be wounded, and if the challenge is failed one of the characters will die.

- Try to survive separately through a  Prowess challenge. Any character who fails the challenge will die.

- Sacrifice yourself to save the family member. The player character will die, the family member will escape unharmed, and the dynasty will gain 250  Renown.

- Sacrifice the family member to survive. The family member will die and the player character will escape unharmed; the player character will lose 350  Prestige and 250  Piety, and gain the  Craven trait.

## Dynastic Cycle


|
| Available only with the All Under Heaven DLC enabled.
 |


The Dynastic Cycle is present in all start dates. A character will become an interloper if it either owns initial de jure territory of Hegemony of China or they are located inside a barony that's both de jure part of Hegemony of China's and currently owned by them. They will become involved if they are either part of Hegemony of China or if they are a tributary who has Palace Access in their contract. Only territory that's initially de jure part of Hegemony of China is affected; newly assimilated empires will not be.


### Eras


Phases of the Dynastic Cycle are called eras. In 1066 it starts in the  Advancement era, in 867 and 1178 it starts in the  Tension era, and in 867 it also starts with 40% progress towards the Division era. The situation will alternate between  Tension and either  Advancement or  Expansion. Which era will be picked depends on the Favored Era, which can be selected by the Hegemon every 20 years at the cost of 250  Influence. When an   Advancement or  Expansion era starts, the Hegemon can spend 225  Legitimacy and 50  Opinion with every member of the Advancement, Expansion and Conservative movement to switch to the other era.


Every 10 years the hegemon and members of a political movement can add 50 Catalyst Progress towards or away from Advancement, Expansion, or Division. This costs 250  Influence and 200  Power for their movement (Pro-Dynasty if the hegemon does it).

### Division


The  Division phase can be reached either through Catalysts during the Tension or Conquest phase, if the hegemon owns less than 30% of China's de jure territory or reaches the  Illegitimate Ruler legitimacy level, or if the Hegemony of China title is destroyed. When the Division phase is reached all  Hegemony titles owned by the ruler of China title are destroyed, the former hegemon suffers an immediate succession, and all its subjects become independent. Every  Kingdom title that is part of it is also destroyed if its holder has less than 100  Movement Power and every  Empire title that is part of it is also destroyed if its holder has less than 350  Movement Power. The Silk Road situation will enter the Hardship phase in every region, and the Attend Children's Examination decision will become unavailable.


As a result of the changes, every previously involved ruler will become an interloper. All interlopers will receive -20%  Taxes and +100%  Legitimacy from completing great projects; will gain  Legitimacy if they appoint characters with  Merit Rank 1-4 as governors, councilors and court positions; and will unlock the Consolidate Land Under Heaven casus belli. The only way for the Division phase to end is by claiming the Mandate of Heaven, which is available to all independent rulers who control more than half of the de jure counties of  Hegemony of China. A ruler who claims the Mandate of Heaven immediately receives the following rewards:


-  Hegemony of China title

- 1000 Treasury

- 1000 Influence

- 2500 Renown

- 25 Control in every county de jure part of Hegemony of China

- Maximum Legitimacy

- Tributaries with the capital in the de jure territory of Hegemony of China become vassals

- Change government to Celestial if not already

- Vassals that don't have Republic, Ecclesiastical or Theocratic government change government to Celestial and gain Merit scaled by their age and skills

- Independent rulers with the capital in the de jure territory of Hegemony of China that have Celestial government, the same heritage or the same dynasty are vassalized

- Son of Heaven permanent modifier

- Choice to change the situation phase to either Advancement or Expansion

- Every courtier with at least 3000 Merit creates a noble family if not part of one

- Councilors are replaced if characters with higher Merit Rank are available


### Political movements

A subject can change their Political Movement every 5 years at the cost of 250  Influence. The hegemon can favor a movement every 20 years at the cost of 250  Influence and 25 Progress towards  Division.


Every Political Movement has a leader, who unlocks the Make Movement Petition decision. When the leader dies the character with most Personal  Movement Power becomes the new leader but they will be replaced if they are defeated in a  Debate activity.

## The Silk Road

The Silk Road situation affects all rulers with their capital in one of the situation regions. There are 6 situation regions, connected in the following order: Occident < Transcaspia and India < Central Asia and Tibet < China. Every region has an innovation assigned and every 12 years a random innovation discovered by the Han culture will be assigned to the China situation and all existing innovations will move westwards. These innovations can be researched normally by cultures with Chinese heritage or the Sinophilic tradition as well as if a ruler of that culture controls the region's hub while the innovation is present in the region. Other rulers can attempt to unlock these innovations by taking the Visit a Silk Road Market decision and passing the skill challenge.


How each ruler is affected depends on the phase in their region and their participant group.

Steppe seasons are situations that affect characters with  Nomadic government present in certain locations if The Great Steppe Game Rule was enabled. The character with the highest  Herd involved in a situation will be considered the Gurkhan, who gains access to the  Unrestricted Dominance law and the Establish Paiza System decision.


There are 5 steppe season situations, depending on where the character's Nomadic capital is located. Every steppe season situation covers multiple regions, and different regions may be affected by different situation phases. The regions can be expanded via the Expand the Steppes decision.

## The Christian Church


The Christian Church is a situation that affects every character whose religion is  Christianity and is located in an existing or potential clerical region. Phases of the situation are called chapters. In 867 the situation starts in the  Fragile Unity chapter, in 1066 it starts in the  Reform chapter, and in 1178 it starts in the  Crusade chapter.


As long as chapter is not  Investiture Controversy heads of rite, cardinals, archbishops, patriarchs, emperors, hegemons and characters whose dynasty finished the  Servants of God legacy can push the situation towards any possible future chapter every 10 years at the cost of 250  Piety. This is 4 times more effective if character is head of faith.

### Before Great Schism


In 867 the situation tracks a value called Schism Progress, which ranges from 0 to 13. It is increased by 1 whenever one of the following hapepns:


- An Ecomenical Council activity voted to remove a tenet or doctrine

- A quarrel between two rites happens before the debates of an Ecumenical Council activity

- A war takes place between two  Kings or above who are involved in the situation

- A holy site of  Roman Rite is converted to  Greek Rite or the other way around

- A holy site of  Roman Rite or  Greek Rite is demoted

- The head of rite of  Roman Rite or  Greek Rite is murdered by a character who practices the other rite


If any of these happen next time the situation enters the  Saeculum Obscurum chapter the Great Schism will begin. When the chapter starts all rulers following the faith with most counties will be given the option to stand with either one rite or both and other rulers following the faith with most counties can be asked to change their stance via a new character interaction. If Schism Progress reaches 13 the option to cause or prevent the Great Schism will be unlocked. Both options have the following requirements:


-  King or above

- The character or a puppet is head of rite

- 4 of the following:
 Paragon of Virtue or above

- Control 1 Holy Site

- Control 2 Holy Sites

- Chose to oppose the schism them the Saeculum Obscurum chapter started

- 7 Other Ecclesiastical rulers following the faith with most counties made the same choice when the chapter started and are either:
Puppet

- Friend or Best Friend

- Lover or Soulmate

- Have a hook on them


The Great Schism can end in three ways:


| **Cause the Great Schism**
| **Prevent the Great Schism**
| **Saeculum Obscurum chapter ends**  |


| Situation enters the Concord chapter  Dynasty gains the Breakers of Communion modifier  Gain the Schismat nickname  Catholicism is created and Roman Rite, Ambrosian Rite, Insularism and Mozarabism join it  Orthodoxy is created and Greek Rite, Georgian Rite and Slavic Rite join it  The pope becomes head of faith of Roman Rite  The ecumenical patriarch becomes head of faith of Greek Rite  Character cannot mend the Great Schism
| Situation enters the Concord chapter  Dynasty gains the Guardians of Unity modifier  Gain the Concordant nickname  If rite is Roman Rite can make the pope head of faith  If rite is Greek Rite can make the ecumenical patriarch head of faith
| Situation enters the Reform chapter  Catholicism is created and Roman Rite, Ambrosian Rite, Insularism and Mozarabism join it  Orthodoxy is created and Greek Rite, Georgian Rite and Slavic Rite join it  The pope becomes head of faith of Roman Rite  The ecumenical patriarch becomes head of faith of Greek Rite  |


Chalcedonianism cannot establish a Head of Faith until the Great Schism happened or is prevented.


### After the Great Schism


In 1066 or 1178 start dates or after the causing the Great Schism has taken place in a 867 start date the  Concord chapter will have the option to mend the Great Schism as long as at least one county follows either a Catholic or an Orthodox rite. Mending the Great Schism has the following effects:


- If faith is Orthodoxy all Catholic rites will become Orthodox rites and if faith is Catholicism all Orthodox rites will become Catholic rites

- If the Great Schism was mended through diplomacy the character's dynasty gains the  Peacemakers of Christendom modifier and if the Great Schism was mended through warfare the character's dynasty gains the  Restorers of Communion modifier

- Character gains 1500 Prestige

- Character gains 1000 Piety

- Dynasty gains 10000 Renown

- Faith gains 50 Fervor

- Gain the Ecumenism nickname

- Rite gains the Ecumenism doctrine if it doesn't have it already

- Every other Christian rite that has the Ecumenism doctrine loses it, loses 25 Fervor, and all its rulers are given the chance to convert
AI characters have a 80% chance to convert if they don't have the  Zealous trait and will always convert if they have the  Cynical trait


- Gain a Holy Legend Seed


This can be done through either diplomacy or warfare.

| **Warfare requirements**  |
