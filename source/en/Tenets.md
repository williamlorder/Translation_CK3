# Tenet

> Source: https://ck3.paradoxwikis.com/Tenets
> License: CC BY-SA 3.0 (Paradox Wikis)

This article has been verified for the current PC version (1.20) of the game.

Tenets represent the issues that a rite takes a stance on. Tenets have different statuses within a rite and can also be adopted a personal tenets if the character has personal tenet slots available. Removing a personal tenet gives  Stress and prevents removing or replacing another personal tenet for 3 years. The piety cost to choose a tenet when creating a new rite depends on what religion it belongs to. The cost of the tenet will also be discounted by 50% if it is present in the original rite.

Tenets often have requirements in order to be made Core tenets but adopting a tenet as a personal tenet does not have any requirements.

## Tenet status

Within a rite every tenet has one of 5 statuses. Heads of faith can change the status of tenets through the Permit a Tenet, Forbid a Tenet, and Enact a Bull decisions. Tenets can be learned from events or the  Study Rite scheme. Only  Christianity starts with Permitted and Prohibited tenets.

## Syncretism

There is a Syncretism tenet for each religion. Syncretism tenets allows characters that have it as core or personal tenet to use artifacts restricted to that religion and gives +15% Scheme Success Chance for  Study Rite schemes, which stacks if the tenet is both core and personal. In addition syncretism tenets give the following effects:

- If used as a core tenet the rite borrows at one  Virtue with a Virtue Weight of 15 and one or two  Sins with a Sin Weight of 10 from another religion, grants +30  Opinion mutually with all characters and counties following it and makes its faith and the rite with the tenet consider each other Astray/Hostile instead of Hostile/Evil, depending on the Theism doctrine. If a rite has a Syncretism core tenet and attempts to convert a county whose Faith matches it, the  County Conversion Speed is reduced by −75%. Each rite can have only one Syncretism tenet.
- If used as a personal tenet all domain counties that follow the tenet's religion gain +10%  Development Growth and +10  Popular Opinion. Rulers also unlock the Invite Religious Exiles decision.

## Tenet popularity

Christianity tracks a value for each tenet called Tenet Popularity, which affects whether AI characters will support it or oppose it during  Ecumenical Council activities as well as the chance that it will be targeted by a Papal Bull decision. If Tenet Popularity reaches 2000 the head of faith will declare it Permitted. Tenet Popularity is calculated by the following:

- +1 per county that follows a rite that has the tenet as core tenet
- +1 per  Baron that has the tenet as personal tenet
- +2 per  Count that has the tenet as personal tenet
- +3 per  Duke that has the tenet as personal tenet
- +4 per  King that has the tenet as personal tenet
- +5 per  Emperor that has the tenet as personal tenet
- +6 per  Hegemon that has the tenet as personal tenet
- +10 per  Monastic Order that has the tenet
- +40 per Evangelize Personal Tenet decision without the third  Servants of God legacy
- +60 per Evangelize Personal Tenet decision with the third  Servants of God legacy
- +40 if the new head of faith has the tenet as a personal tenet
- +40 if the tenet becomes  Permitted
- -100 if the tenet becomes  Prohibited
- -100 if tenet is a core tenet and its rite is declared heresy
- Tenet popularity can also be changed by the outcome of  Ecumenical Council activities
