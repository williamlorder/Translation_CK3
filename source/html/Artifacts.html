Inventory artifacts

Artifacts are objects unique in the world that give various bonuses to the character using them and are always inherited by the primary heir. Artifacts are divided into two categories: inventory artifacts and court artifacts.

  • Inventory artifacts are worn by a character via their inventory. Each character can wear up to 9 artifacts: a crown, a journal, a weapon, an armor, a regalia, a brooch, and 3 trinkets.
  • Court artifacts are displayed in a character's Royal Court. Each court can display up to 15 artifacts: 3 large wall ornaments, 3 small wall ornaments, 2 pieces of furniture, 2 book items, 4 pedestal items, and a throne.

Artifacts may be given as gifts to other characters. This will grant +20  Opinion if the receiving character can use the artifact and +5  Opinion if it cannot.

Inventory artifacts that are not worn as well as court artifacts have a 30% (45% if rarity is Common) chance to be damaged and a 30% chance to be stolen if the realm capital is besieged. If an artifact is lost during a siege its previous owner will gain a claim on it. All house heads will have a claim on the Dynasty Banner of their dynasty. Having a claim allows a character to demand the return of their artifact although accepting requires sufficiently superior military strength. More importantly, artifact claims allow the claimant to use the Artifact Claim War casus belli or challenge the artifact owner to a duel for the artifact. Rulers who have the Accomplished Forger perk can also buy claims on artifacts, with the cost depending on the rarity of the artifact.

Available only with the Coronations DLC enabled.

Rulers with the Uncrowned law cannot use Regalia artifacts if their religion is Hinduism, Buddhism, Jainism, Hausa, Finno-Ugric or Slavic and cannot use Crown artifacts if their religion is any other. But owning such an artifact is necessary to increase Coronation activity options.

Rarity

All artifacts have a Rarity, which determines the artifact's Durability, cost to repair and how much  Gold is obtained if the artifact is manually destroyed.

All artifacts have a Durability, which represents the integrity of the artifact. When it reaches zero, the artifact is destroyed. If a non-historical artifact is inherited by someone who is not a close family member, it will also lose Durability. All artifacts will lose 1 Durability each year except for artifacts displayed in the royal court. Durability loss can be reduced by employing an Antiquarian, which also enables artifacts to be reforged. Reforging an artifact costs  Gold, but will replenish its Durability. When reforging an artifact, its owner will always have the option to turn it into a Pedestal Item artifact, if it's not one already. Doing so will also change an artifact's effects. As adventurers cannot employ an Antiquarian, they cannot reforge an artifact, but can still repair one.

The cost of repairing an artifact is influenced by the remaining durability. Base repair cost also depends on an internal not visible ingame value calculation, some famed artifacts will count as illustrious for repairs for example. Base repair costs in the table below are a guideline.

Creating artifacts

For rulers the main way of obtaining artifacts is via the Commission Artifact decision and for adventurers the main way of obtaining artifacts is by visiting City holdings and buying them with  Gold. Rulers can also use the Commission Epic decision, which will create a Book artifact if the character has a royal court and a Journal artifact if the character does not have a royal court.

If a character wins a game of chess there is a 5% chance to gain a trinket artifact called Lucky Go Board if the character is in Eastern Asia and Lucky Chess Board if the character is somewhere else. The chance is increased to 25% if the game was played for a county. When offered, the artifact can also be destroyed to lose 10  Stress or improved for a moderate amount of  Gold.

If the  Tours and Tournaments DLC is installed, winning  Grand Tournament contests will reward artifacts. Their rarity depends on the Prizes activity option. All contests can award trinket or pedestal item artifacts, but certain contests can also award weapon, armor or bow artifacts.

If the  All Under Heaven DLC is installed, tributaries of China can obtain a Fine Seal of Investiture regalia by paying tribute while they have sufficient Imperial Grace.

If the  Roads to Power DLC is installed a Golden Coat of the Swan armor can be obtained via the Knight of the Swan decision.

If the  By God Alone DLC is installed pedestal items can also be created by extracting a relic from a canonized character.

If a character has a Royal Court artifacts can be obtained the following ways:

  • When a pet dies there will be the option to pay 10-20  Gold to gain a small wall ornament.
  • Book artifacts can be obtained via the University Visit activity.
  • If a character wins a battle against a Noble opponent there is a chance to gain a Warbanner large wall ornament. Alternatively the artifact can be destroyed to gain 75  Prestige.
  • If a character has the  Arbitrary,  Deceitful,  Callous,  Sadistic or  Vengeful trait executing a prisoner has a 25% chance to be offered a Skull pedestal item. If the prisoner was a Nemesis the option will always be given. If the ruler does not have an Executioner it will lose -30  Opinion with the Personal Physician or Antiquarian.
  • If the  Royal Court DLC is installed, all characters will have a House Banner large wall ornament. If their last  Kingdom or above title the character who took the last title will get the artifact but if they're not part of the same house only the Court Grandeur bonus will be given.
  • If the  All Under Heaven DLC is installed, all independent rulers with  Celestial,  Meritocratic and  Meritocratic Khanate government as well as the owner of the Chrysanthemum Throne title will have an Imperial Seal pedestal item.
  • If the  All Under Heaven DLC is installed, tributaries of rulers with  Mandala government unlock the Create Bunga Mas decision, which can create a pedestal item.
  • If the  Friends and Foes DLC is installed, when a nemesis dies characters with the  Callous,  Sadistic or  Vengeful trait can gain a Skull Goblet pedestal item. The option will not be available if the nemesis' rite has the Sky Burials funerary tradition.

Hunt trophies

Successful  Hunt activities will grant trinket artifacts. If the character has a Royal Court and the activity variant was not Falconry, the artifacts can also be wall ornaments. These artifacts will always give monthly  Prestige and depending on their rarity will also have additional effects.

  • Foxes, gazelles, hares, roes and non-exotic birds will always be Common
  • Hyenas, lynxes, wolves and exotic birds have a 97% chance to be Common and a 3% chance to be Masterwork
  • Antelopes, aurochs, bisons, reindeers and stags have a 50% chance to be Common and a 50% chance to be Masterwork
  • Bears, leopards, lions and tigers have an 85% chance to be Masterwork and a 15% chance to be Famed
  • Boars, elks and harts have a 33% chance to be Masterwork and a 67% chance to be Famed
  • If the activity variant was Legendary then the rarity will be doubled
  • A few rare events during the activity can also increase the rarity

Inspirations

Available only with the Royal Court DLC or the Roads to Power DLC enabled.

Landless characters occasionally gain an Inspiration, allowing them to work on creating an artifact. Characters older than 13 can sponsor the inspirations with  Gold, with the cost depending on how skilled the inspired character is. Once an inspired character is financed they will start working on the artifact. The artifact's effects will depend on the inspired character's traits. The artifact rarity is decided by an average of the relevant skills plus modifiers from some cultures, buildings or events. There are three ways for characters to gain inspirations:

  • Inspired characters can randomly appear in royal courts. The greater a court's Grandeur the higher the chance that an inspired character will appear.
  • Followers of adventurers who have the Inspiration is Everywhere lifestyle perk can randomly gain inspirations.
  • Entourage members may gain a Book inspiration if liege adopted Scholasticism as a personal tenet.

There are multiple types of Inspiration, which determine what type of artifact will be created. If an inspiration can create more than one type of artifact there's a small chance the sponsor will be allowed to choose what sort of artifact they want created.

Icons

Available only with the Roads to Power DLC enabled.

Icons are artifacts that can be created via the Commission Icon decision. There are three artifact options to choose from when taking the decision:

All characters can choose to create a Trinket artifact. For landed rulers the artifact will have Masterwork rarity and provide +0.3 or +0.4 monthly  Piety. For landless rulers there is a small chance the artifact will have Common rarity and provide +0.1 or +0.2 monthly  Piety.

Rulers with a royal court an choose to create a Large Wall Ornament or Small Wall Ornament artifact. The artifact will have two of the following bonuses:

  • Monthly piety
  • Temple holding and building construction cost reduction
  • Zealot vassal tax contribution
  • Zealot vassal opinion
  • Clergy opinion

Sanctifying artifacts

Artifacts can be sanctified by the religious affairs councilor or the head of faith. This can be done every 5 years but the cooldown is not shared between religious affairs councilor and the head of faith unless they are the same character. Sanctifying an artifact costs  Piety and  Gold or  Treasury that scales with the artifact's rarity, makes the artifact a holy relic, and adds to its effects +0.1 Monthly Piety and +5 Zealot Vassal Opinion. Other characters can ask the head of faith to sanctify artifacts but this requires a Faithful or above level of devotion.

Book and Pedestal Item artifacts can always be sanctified. Other artifacts may be sanctified depending on what core tenets the character's rite has. Only artifacts of Masterwork rarity or above can be sanctified. If rite has the Asceticism, Mendicant Preaching or Vows of Poverty core tenet Book and Pedestal Item artifacts of Common rarity can also be sanctified but Illustrious rarity artifacts of any kind cannot be sanctified. Weapons that have been merged with a holy relic ignore all requirements and can always be sanctified.

Historical artifacts have mostly fixed effects. If they are lost or the required title does not exist in the chosen start date they may be discovered randomly through Adventure inspirations. If a character obtains an artifact it lacks the requirements to use only the Court Grandeur bonus will be given. Artifacts that need a certain religion can be used if the rite has the syncretism core tenet for the required religion.

Travel itineraries

Available only with the Wandering Nobles DLC enabled.

Characters who have the Charts & Itineraries lifestyle perk can take the Create Travel Itinerary decision to create a journal artifact. When the decision is taken the character will have to choose what point of interest to write about. There will be up to 4 options depending on whether their type of point of interest has been visited. The bonuses provided scale with the amount of experience the  Traveler trait has.

  • Choosing to write about points of interest within the domain will bring the option to focus on resources or defense.
    • Focusing on resources will give Monthly Development in terrain matching one of the baronies with the point of interest.
    • Focusing on defense will give either Controlled Territory Defender Advantage or Men-at-Arms Maintenance Reduction.
  • Choosing to write about a point of interest that is a vassal capital will bring the option to focus on control, aid or secrets.
    • Focusing on control will give  Dread Gain.
    • Focusing on aid will give either Powerful Vassal Councilor Tax Contribution or Prestige per Powerful Vassal on Council.
    • Focusing on secrets will give either Initial Hostile Scheme Success Chance or Scheme Secrecy.
  • Choosing to write about a point of interest that contains a special building will bring the option to focus on the travel or the destination.
    • Focusing on the travel will give Travel Speed and Travel Safety.
    • Focusing on the destination will give Monthly Experience to the lifestyle that received experience when the barony was visited. If the point of interest was a holy site it will instead give Monthly  Piety. If the point of interest was a natural feature it will instead give Hunter Trait Experience Gain.
  • Choosing to write about a point of interest that is a neighbor capital will bring the option to focus on the realm or the people. Characters with the  Eccentric trait can also choose to write about a mythical creature.
    • Focusing on realm will give Monthly Prestige if the character does not have a royal court and Court Grandeur if the character has a royal court.
    • Focusing on people will give either Independent Ruler Opinion or Diplomacy.
    • Focusing on a mythical creature will give either Renown or Dynasty Opinion.

5 days later you will have to choose which aspect of the journey to write about.

  • You will always have the option to write about the terrain of the point of interest. The artifact will have the following bonus:
    • 65% chance to give +5  Advantage in that terrain
    • 35% chance to give +8  Advantage in that terrain
  • If the point of interest county had below 50  Control or was at war you will have the option to write about the danger. The artifact will have the following bonus:
    • 25% chance to give +3 Defender Advantage in controlled territory
    • 25% chance to give +2% Enemy Fatal Casualties
