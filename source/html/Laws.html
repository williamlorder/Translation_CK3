Laws are royal decrees that have a vital effect on the development of the realm. Decreasing the level of a law is free and can be done at any point but increasing the level of a law costs  Prestige.

Borders Wars Policy

The Border Wars Policy law dictates if the liege will join its vassals' defensive wars against foreign rulers. They law is set automatically and cannot be changed.

  • If the title rank is  Hegemony, realm size is 160 or higher, the country is in a civil war, or House Unity is Antagonistic, the law will be set to Conditional Join. The liege will not automatically join its vassals' defensive wars but if the war lasts 3 years they can use the Protect Vassal interaction to do so. Certain factions will also gain Discontent twice faster.
  • If the title rank is not  Hegemony, realm size is is less than 160, the country is not in a civil war and House Unity is not Antagonistic, the law will be set to Guaranteed Protection. The liege will automatically join its vassals' defensive wars.

Administrative laws

Available only with the Roads to Power DLC enabled.

Administrative governments have access to the following laws. Changing them costs 200  Prestige, increased by 1 per Realm Size and reduced by 1 per  Learning.

Provincial War Declaration law dictates which governors can wage external wars and requires the Imperial Bureaucracy law to be at the same level or higher.

  • At level 1 governors can declare external wars
  • At level 2 only Frontier and Naval governors can declare external wars
  • At level 3 only Frontier and Naval governors can declare external wars and require permission from the emperor
  • At level 4 governors cannot declare external wars

Provincial Border Policy law dictates which governors can use the Boundary Dispute and Subsume Governorship political schemes and requires the Imperial Bureaucracy law to be one level or more above.

  • At level 1 governors can use the schemes
  • At level 2 governors can use the schemes if they belong to a Powerful Family or Dominant Family and reduces the  Opinion of administrative vassals by -5
  • At level 3 governors cannot use the schemes and reduces the  Opinion of administrative vassals by -10

Celestial laws

Available only with the All Under Heaven DLC enabled.

Independent rulers with  Celestial government have access to the following laws. Changing them costs 200  Prestige, increased by 1 per Realm Size and reduced by 1 per  Learning.

Retirement Age law dictates the retirement age of governors. Can be set to 50, 60, 70, 80 or 90. Governors who are above retirement age have their candidate score reduced and unlock the Retire from Heading the House decision and their liege can use the the Retire Governor interaction on them.

Army Command Structure law decides how are men-at-arms owned by military governors.

Hegemony of China laws

The Hegemony of China unlocks two additional laws as long as its government remains  Celestial.

General Secretariat Control law dictates which councilor will be the Grand Secretariat diarch. Can be set to Grand Chancellor, Grand Marshal or Imperial Censor. Changing the law does not have any cost but can only be done while the Dynastic Cycle situation is in the Advancement phase or the Advancement Movement is the favored movement. AI hegemons will always keep the law to Grand Chancellor unless a human player is one of the other possible councilors.

Candidate Score Focus law dictates whether the level of fame and level of splendor affect the candidate scores and only affects characters who have the same government. Changing the law costs 1000  Influence and scaled  Treasury. The law initially starts set to Merit and changing it to Family requires the Conservative Movement to be the Favored Movement and have 5 Ministries and 3 Powerful Family house heads.

Imperial Policy

Available only with the All Under Heaven DLC enabled.

Independent emperors with  Ritsuryō or  Sōryō government have access to the Imperial Policy law, which affects both themselves and their vassals. Changing the law 500  Prestige. Vassals can change the law by forming an Imperial Policy faction and getting the liege to accept its ultimatum or win the civil war.

Coronation

Available only with the Coronations DLC enabled.

As long as the Coronation Laws game rule has not been set to No Coronation Laws, every  King and above who doesn't have  Ecclesiastical or Theocratic government will have the Coronation law. Unlike other laws, it cannot be changed manually and is instead increased automatically upon completing a Coronation activity. Every new ruler will start with the law set to the lowest level and depending on game rules increasing the title rank to  Empire or  Hegemony may decrease it as well.

In realms with a Ceremonial Monarchy neither monarch will have the Coronation law unless they are the same character. Rulers with  Ritsuryō or  Sōryō government will only have the the Coronation law if they are the top liege.

Cardinal Benefices

Available only with the By God Alone DLC enabled.

Cardinals have access to the Cardinal Benefices law, which grants the Cardinal additional  Treasury from the head of faith. The law can be turned off at any point.
